What we do

PPS is fractional HR systems support. We build the operating system so your team can run day-to-day people operations consistently — without adding headcount.

Design

Define ownership, workflows, and "the way we do it here" — the minimum viable structure your business actually needs.

Implement

Put the system in place: templates, checklists, manager guides, tooling setup, and clean handoffs your team can run.

Maintain

Keep it usable as you grow. Tune the system, keep managers aligned, and prevent drift before it becomes a problem.

Systems-first, in plain English

We don't "take over HR." We create simple, repeatable processes for hiring, onboarding, performance, and documentation — so fewer things become emergencies and more things run on autopilot.
