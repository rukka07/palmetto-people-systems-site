What we do

PPS is fractional HR systems support. We build the operating system so your team can run day-to-day people operations consistently.

Design

Define ownership, workflows, and “the way we do it here” with the minimum structure your business needs.

Implement

Put the system in place: templates, checklists, manager guides, tooling setup, and clean handoffs.

Maintain

Keep it usable as you grow. Tune the system, keep managers aligned, and prevent drift.

Systems-first (plain English)

We don’t “take over HR.” We create simple, repeatable ways your business handles hiring, onboarding, performance basics, and documentation— so fewer things become emergencies.

Built for how your business actually works

Manufacturing and warehousing environments have different constraints. PPS is designed for that reality.

Operational realities

  • Shift work, hourly populations, and high-volume hiring
  • Supervisor-led teams with limited admin time
  • Safety expectations, documentation, and training needs
  • Fast changes: headcount swings, new lines, new contracts

What “better” looks like

  • Hiring flow that’s consistent across supervisors
  • Onboarding that doesn’t rely on tribal knowledge
  • Documentation that exists before you need it
  • Clear escalation paths (so it doesn’t all hit the owner)
